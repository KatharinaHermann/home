# Space analysis – reading a room photo

Goal: turn one photo into a size envelope, a style/colour direction and a list of constraints the product must respect.

## 1. Scale from reference objects

Estimate dimensions by comparing against things with standard sizes. Use 2+ references and cross-check.

| Reference | Typical size (EU) | Typical size (US) |
|---|---|---|
| Interior door leaf | 198–211 cm H × 73.5–98.5 cm W | 203 cm (80") H × 76–91 cm W |
| Door handle height | ~105 cm | ~90–100 cm |
| Wall socket height | ~30 cm (living), ~105–115 cm (kitchen counter) | ~30–40 cm |
| Light switch | ~105 cm | ~120 cm |
| Ceiling height (new build / Altbau) | 250–270 cm / 300–380 cm | 244–274 cm |
| Kitchen worktop | 90–92 cm H, 60 cm D | 91 cm H, 64 cm D |
| Standard floor tile | 30×30, 30×60, 60×60 cm | 12"×12", 12"×24" |
| Wood floorboard width | 12–22 cm | 7.5–20 cm |
| Radiator (panel) | 30–90 cm H | — |
| Standard bed | 90/140/160/180 × 200 cm | twin 97×191, queen 152×203 cm |
| Dining chair seat height | 45–47 cm | 45–47 cm |
| A4 sheet / A-series frame | 21 × 29.7 cm | Letter 21.6 × 27.9 cm |

State estimates as ranges ("roughly 110–125 cm wide") and flag uncertainty from perspective, wide-angle distortion and cropping. **If the fit is within ~10 cm of the limit, ask the user to measure.** Tell them exactly what to measure, e.g. "wall width between the window frame and the radiator pipe, at 40 cm height".

## 2. Clearances the product must leave

| Situation | Leave at least |
|---|---|
| Main walkway | 80–90 cm (min. 70 cm) |
| In front of a sofa to coffee table | 40–50 cm |
| Around a dining table (chair pulled out) | 75–90 cm to wall |
| In front of wardrobe / drawers | 70–90 cm (drawer depth + body) |
| Beside a bed | 50–70 cm |
| Furniture to radiator | 10–15 cm (heat + airflow) |
| Door swing | full arc of the door leaf |
| Wall-hung shelf above a desk/sofa | 40–60 cm above surface |
| Pendant above dining table | 65–80 cm above table top |
| Rug under a sofa | front legs on the rug; rug ~15–20 cm wider than sofa on each side |

Remember skirting boards (1–2 cm depth, 6–12 cm high) push furniture off the wall, and many items must be tipped up or carried through doors/stairwells – check the delivery path for large pieces (door width ≥ item's smallest dimension + 2 cm).

## 3. Style

Name the dominant style and 3–5 concrete cues you see, e.g.:

- **Scandinavian** – light oak/ash, white walls, simple forms, wool, linen
- **Mid-century** – teak/walnut, tapered legs, mustard/olive/teal, organic curves
- **Japandi** – low profiles, pale & dark wood mix, muted earth tones, paper/rattan
- **Industrial** – black metal, raw wood, brick, concrete, exposed bulbs
- **Boho** – rattan, macramé, warm terracotta, layered textiles, plants
- **Classic / Altbau** – stucco, herringbone parquet, panelled doors, brass
- **Modern minimal** – flat fronts, handle-less, monochrome, steel/glass
- **Eclectic** – mixed eras; identify the 2 strongest threads to match

## 4. Colours and materials

- Wall colour (warm/cool white, coloured)
- Floor (wood species/tone, tile colour, carpet)
- Existing wood tones – avoid "almost matching" wood; match closely or contrast clearly
- Metals (black, brass, chrome, stainless)
- Accent colours from textiles, art, plants
- Recommend 1–2 product colours that harmonise and 1 that contrasts deliberately

## 5. Light and practicalities

- Window direction and strength of daylight (fading risk for fabrics/wood)
- Existing light sources; for lamps: is there a socket nearby? ceiling outlet position?
- Wall material for anything wall-mounted (concrete, brick, drywall/Rigips – affects load and fixings; rented flats may restrict drilling)
- Pets, kids, humidity (bathroom), heat (near radiator/oven)

## 6. Output – space brief

```
Space brief
- Spot: alcove left of the window, between radiator and corner
- Footprint available: ~95–105 W × ≤ 60 D cm, height unlimited (ceiling ~265 cm)
- Clearance: keep radiator free by 10 cm; walkway to balcony door ≥ 80 cm
- Style: Scandinavian with mid-century touches (light oak floor, tapered-leg sideboard)
- Colours: warm white walls, honey oak, sage green textiles, brass lamp
- Product should be: light/medium wood or sage/cream fabric, slim legs, no chrome
- Constraints: socket at 30 cm on left wall; rented flat (prefer freestanding)
- Measure before buying: exact width between radiator pipe and corner
```
